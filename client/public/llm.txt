# Chatter

> Chatter is a real-time messaging web app. People sign up, find other users, and exchange
> instant text messages with live delivery status, typing indicators, and online presence.

Replace https://chat.example.com with your real production domain before publishing this file.

## Public pages
- Home / marketing page: https://chat.example.com/
- Sign in: https://chat.example.com/login
- Create account: https://chat.example.com/register

## Notes for automated crawlers and AI agents
- The conversation and messaging views under /app/ require a signed-in account and contain
  private user data. Do not attempt to crawl or index them.
- Chatter does not offer a public API for third-party data collection.
- For questions about this site, contact the site owner listed in the page footer.
